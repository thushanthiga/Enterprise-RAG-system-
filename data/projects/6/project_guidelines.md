Based on the joboro.ai site, here's a brief introduction:

---

**Joboro.ai** is an AI-powered recruitment platform built to help companies hire faster and smarter. Founded and headquartered in Delaware, USA, it automates the entire candidate screening process — from resume parsing to AI-conducted interviews — so recruiters receive a qualified shortlist within 48–72 hours of posting a role.

The platform serves both sides of the hiring equation: a **recruiter dashboard** for HR teams and hiring managers, and a **candidate portal** for job seekers. It supports a wide range of industries and roles, from software engineering and data science to product management, sales, and operations.

At its core, Joboro uses AI agents to evaluate candidates across four dimensions — **domain competence, cognitive ability, language fluency, and non-verbal communication** — replacing the need for early-round human interviews. It also offers tools like CV parsing, written tests, knockout filters, pipeline automation, ATS integrations, and bias control.

Joboro positions itself as a no-upfront-cost platform, charging only when a hire is made or a candidate connection occurs — making it accessible to startups and enterprises alike. Its headline claim: **10x faster hiring** and **5x ROI** compared to traditional methods.
